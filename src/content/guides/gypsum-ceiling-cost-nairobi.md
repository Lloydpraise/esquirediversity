## What affects the price
- Ceiling area
- Flat ceiling or designed one with trays and bulkheads
- Number and type of lights
- Room height and access

## How to read a ceiling quote
An itemised quote (BoQ) should list boards, framing, cornices, labour, lighting and finishing separately, so you can compare like with like.
